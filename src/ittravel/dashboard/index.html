Stop account takeovers before they cause damage.

If a user signs in from New York and then 10 minutes later from Tokyo, it is physically impossible. Aegis scores every login with models trained on 31.3M logins from the RBA dataset (account takeover and attack-IP risk) and adds a physical impossible-travel check on the speed between consecutive logins.
