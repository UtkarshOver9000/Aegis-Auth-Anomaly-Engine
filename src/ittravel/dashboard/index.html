      Data and accuracy

      Alibi mixes two kinds of data. The threat data (breaches, malware servers, criminal networks, ransomware, flaws, censorship) is downloaded from public sources every day; the table below shows how old each copy is. The login models are older: they were trained once on the RBA dataset, 31.3 million logins from 4.3 million users between February 2020 and February 2021. Its authors built each value from real logins at a Norwegian sign-in service and changed the values to protect privacy, and its cities are random.
